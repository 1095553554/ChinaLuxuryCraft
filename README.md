---

## About

ChinaLuxuryCraft is an open-source project dedicated to documenting, preserving, and promoting China's traditional master craftsmanship, intangible cultural heritage, and artisan culture.

This repository aims to build a structured, multilingual knowledge base covering the history, materials, tools, production processes, representative works, cultural significance, and modern applications of Chinese traditional crafts.

Unlike conventional museum collections, the focus of this project is not only the finished artworks, but also the knowledge, techniques, and craftsmanship behind them.

ChinaLuxuryCraft 是一个开源知识库项目，致力于记录、整理、传播中国传统高级工艺与非物质文化遗产。

项目不仅关注成品本身，更关注：

- 工艺流程
- 制作技法
- 材料体系
- 工具使用
- 历史沿革
- 匠人传承
- 文化价值

通过数字化方式保存并传播中国传统工艺文化。

---

## Vision

Many traditional Chinese crafts have survived for hundreds or even thousands of years.

However, the knowledge behind them is often scattered across books, research papers, museums, documentaries, and oral traditions.

This project aims to create an open and structured repository where anyone can learn, contribute, and explore the beauty of Chinese craftsmanship.

我们的目标：

- Digital Preservation（数字化保护）
- Knowledge Sharing（知识共享）
- Cultural Promotion（文化传播）
- Educational Resources（教育资源）
- Future AI Applications（未来AI应用）

## Project Scope

The repository gradually covers the following craft categories:

### Ceramics

- Longquan Celadon（龙泉青瓷）
- Tang Sancai（唐三彩）
- Jingdezhen Porcelain（景德镇陶瓷）
- Yixing Zisha（宜兴紫砂）

### Textile & Embroidery

- Yunjin Brocade（云锦）
- Su Embroidery（苏绣）
- Shu Embroidery（蜀绣）
- Xiang Embroidery（湘绣）

### Seal & Calligraphy Arts

- Longquan Ink Paste（龙泉印泥）
- Seal Engraving（篆刻）
- Chinese Calligraphy（书法）

### Lacquerware

- Fuzhou Lacquerware（福州脱胎漆器）
- Yangzhou Lacquerware（扬州漆器）

### Traditional Architecture

- Mortise and Tenon（榫卯）
- Wooden Arch Bridges（木拱桥）

### Others

- Traditional Papermaking
- Ink Making
- Tea Craft
- Incense Craft
- Musical Instruments
