# 材料与釉料 | Materials and Glazes

## 中文

### 官方资料明确提到的原料

中国非物质文化遗产网介绍，龙泉青瓷传统烧制使用龙泉一带的瓷土、紫金土、石灰石和石英等原料。传统实践还涉及家传或师承体系中的配料经验。

### 材料在工艺中的作用

- **坯体原料**：经粉碎、淘洗、陈腐和练泥后用于制坯。
- **釉料原料**：经选择、处理与配制后形成青釉体系。
- **木柴**：官方流程记录传统龙窑以木柴烧成。
- **匣钵及耐火材料**：用于装匣和装窑，帮助器物在烧成过程中获得相对稳定的空间与保护。

### 需要谨慎记录的内容

青釉颜色与质感不仅由某一种原料决定，还与原料组成、釉层、坯体、窑炉、烧成制度和窑内气氛相关。本项目暂不公布来源不明的“标准配方”。后续如记录具体配方，应至少注明：

- 信息来源或传承人
- 调研时间和地点
- 具体窑口或工作室
- 原料产地和处理方式
- 试片或实物验证结果
- 是否获得公开授权

## English

### Materials Named by Official Sources

The China Intangible Cultural Heritage website identifies porcelain stone or clay, zijintu, limestone, and quartz from the Longquan region among the materials used in the traditional process. Material preparation may also rely on knowledge transmitted within families or apprenticeship lineages.

### Functions Within the Process

- **Body materials:** Crushed, washed, aged, and kneaded before forming.
- **Glaze materials:** Selected, processed, and blended to create the celadon glaze system.
- **Firewood:** Official descriptions record wood firing in the traditional dragon kiln.
- **Saggars and refractory materials:** Used during loading to provide separation and protection in the kiln.

### Information That Requires Careful Verification

Glaze color and texture are not determined by one ingredient alone. They also depend on composition, glaze thickness, clay body, kiln design, firing schedule, and kiln atmosphere. This repository does not publish unverified “standard formulas.” Any future formula record should identify its source, location, workshop, raw-material origin, testing evidence, and publication permission.
