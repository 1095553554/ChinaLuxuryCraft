# 非遗与传承 | Heritage and Safeguarding

## 中文

### 非遗身份

- **2006年**：龙泉青瓷烧制技艺列入第一批国家级非物质文化遗产名录。
- **2009年**：龙泉青瓷传统烧制技艺列入联合国教科文组织人类非物质文化遗产代表作名录。
- **项目类别**：传统手工艺或传统技艺。
- **核心社区**：官方项目资料列出浙江省龙泉市及相关博物馆、行业协会、传承基地和传承人群体。

### 保护的对象

非遗保护的核心并不是仅保存某一件青瓷，而是让以下内容能够持续存在并传承：

- 原料识别与处理经验
- 手工成型和修坯能力
- 釉料配制与施釉知识
- 装窑、控火与判断窑况的经验
- 器型、纹样和审美传统
- 家族、师徒和院校等传承方式
- 与社区生活、生产和文化认同之间的联系

### 当代发展

当代龙泉青瓷同时面对两个任务：保持传统技艺的连续性，以及回应现代生活和设计需求。仓库后续将分别记录“传统复原”“传承性创作”和“现代设计转化”，避免把三者混为一谈。

### 开源记录的边界

- 尊重匠人的署名与知识贡献。
- 不公开未经授权的商业秘密或家传配方。
- 不把短视频的简化叙述替代专业工艺档案。
- 不使用无法确认版权和出处的高清图片。
- 对争议性历史观点保留不同来源和证据。

## English

### Heritage Status

- **2006:** Included in China's first national intangible cultural heritage list.
- **2009:** Inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.
- **Domain:** Traditional craftsmanship.
- **Core community:** Official records identify Longquan, Zhejiang, together with museums, associations, transmission bases, and practitioners.

### What Is Safeguarded

Safeguarding is not limited to preserving individual ceramic objects. It supports the continued transmission of:

- Knowledge of raw materials and their preparation
- Hand-forming and trimming skills
- Glaze preparation and application
- Kiln loading, fire control, and kiln judgment
- Forms, motifs, and aesthetic traditions
- Family, apprenticeship, and institutional transmission
- Connections between the craft, community life, production, and cultural identity

### Contemporary Development

Contemporary Longquan celadon must maintain continuity while responding to modern life and design. Future documentation will distinguish historical reconstruction, tradition-based creation, and modern design adaptation.

### Boundaries of Open Documentation

- Credit artisans and knowledge holders.
- Do not publish unauthorized trade secrets or family formulas.
- Do not substitute simplified short-video narration for professional craft records.
- Do not reuse images whose copyright or provenance cannot be verified.
- Preserve multiple sourced viewpoints where historical interpretations differ.
