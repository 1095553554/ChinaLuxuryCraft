# 工具与设施 | Tools and Facilities

## 中文

龙泉青瓷制作横跨泥料处理、成型、修整、装饰、施釉、装烧和检验。不同工作室的工具会有差异，本页先建立可核验的工具分类，而不把示意图中的工具当作统一标准。

### 泥料处理

- 粉碎与筛分设备
- 淘洗、沉淀和储泥容器
- 练泥工具或设备

### 成型与修坯

- 陶车或拉坯机
- 修坯刀、刮片等修整工具
- 模具或辅助成型工具
- 晾坯架和干燥空间

### 装饰与施釉

- 刻划、印花等装饰工具
- 盛釉容器
- 浸釉、荡釉、涂釉或喷釉相关工具

### 装窑与烧成

- 匣钵、垫饼和其他窑具
- 龙窑或其他用于龙泉青瓷生产的窑炉
- 木柴及投柴、观察、控火工具

### 检验与记录

- 尺寸与外观检查工具
- 釉色、变形、开裂、针孔等缺陷记录
- 窑次、位置、配方和结果档案

> 待补充：工具实拍图、传统名称、尺寸、材质、使用演示和授权来源。

## English

Longquan celadon production spans clay preparation, forming, trimming, decoration, glazing, kiln loading, firing, and inspection. Tools vary by workshop. This page therefore establishes a verifiable classification rather than presenting one illustration as a universal standard.

### Clay Preparation

- Crushing and sieving equipment
- Washing, settling, and clay-storage vessels
- Clay-kneading tools or machinery

### Forming and Trimming

- Potter's wheel
- Trimming knives, ribs, and related tools
- Moulds and auxiliary forming tools
- Drying racks and controlled drying space

### Decoration and Glazing

- Carving, incising, and impressed-decoration tools
- Glaze containers
- Tools for dipping, pouring, brushing, or spraying glaze

### Loading and Firing

- Saggars, setters, and other kiln furniture
- Dragon kiln or other kilns used in Longquan celadon production
- Firewood and tools for stoking, observation, and fire control

### Inspection and Documentation

- Dimensional and visual inspection tools
- Records of glaze color, deformation, cracking, pinholes, and other defects
- Firing batch, kiln position, material, and outcome records
