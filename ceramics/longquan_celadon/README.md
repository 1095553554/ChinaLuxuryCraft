# 龙泉青瓷 | Longquan Celadon

> 千年窑火，温润如玉。  
> A millennium of kiln fire, a jade-like beauty.

## 中文简介

龙泉青瓷以温润如玉的青釉、含蓄典雅的造型和深厚的烧制传统闻名，是中国青瓷的重要代表。这里所记录的核心对象，不只是作为器物的“龙泉青瓷”，更是围绕原料选择、泥料处理、成型、修坯、装饰、素烧、施釉、装匣、装窑和烧成而形成的知识、技能与传承体系。

龙泉青瓷传统烧制技艺于2006年列入第一批国家级非物质文化遗产名录，并于2009年列入联合国教科文组织人类非物质文化遗产代表作名录。官方资料将其历史概括为已有1700余年，并特别指出粉青、梅子青厚釉瓷所体现的淡雅、含蓄、敦厚与宁静之美。

本目录从公开资料、博物馆资源、影像作品和学术文献出发，以中英文方式逐步整理龙泉青瓷的历史、材料、工具、制作流程、经典器型、非遗保护及当代发展。当前内容为首版知识框架，不提供未经传承人或可靠文献验证的秘方、精确配比和烧成参数。

## English Introduction

Longquan celadon is renowned for its jade-like green glaze, restrained forms, and long-established firing tradition. This directory documents not only finished ceramic objects, but also the knowledge and skills surrounding material selection, clay preparation, forming, trimming, decoration, biscuit firing, glazing, loading, kiln firing, and intergenerational transmission.

The traditional firing technology of Longquan celadon was included in China's first national list of intangible cultural heritage in 2006 and inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2009. Official Chinese heritage sources describe a history of more than 1,700 years and highlight the quiet, restrained beauty of the celebrated pale bluish-green and plum-green thick glazes.

This is an evolving bilingual knowledge base built from public heritage records, museum resources, audiovisual works, and research literature. The initial version establishes a documentation framework. It does not publish unverified secret formulas, exact material ratios, or firing parameters.

## 信息原则 | Documentation Principles

- 优先使用联合国教科文组织、中国非物质文化遗产网、国家图书馆、故宫博物院和地方政府或博物馆资料。
- 明确区分官方记录、学术研究、匠人口述和内容创作者的二次讲述。
- 图片和视频只使用获得授权、开放许可或允许引用的材料。
- 对配方、温度、时长等技术参数标明来源和适用窑口，避免把个别实践写成统一标准。

- Prefer UNESCO, official Chinese heritage databases, national institutions, museums, and government sources.
- Distinguish institutional records, academic research, artisan testimony, and creator commentary.
- Use only properly licensed, authorized, or lawfully quoted media.
- Attribute technical parameters to a specific source and workshop rather than presenting them as universal rules.
