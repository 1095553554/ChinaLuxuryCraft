# 图像资料 | Gallery

## 中文

本页当前不直接复制网络图片。首版先建立图像目录和版权字段，待确认授权后再加入仓库。

### 建议的图像分组

1. **代表性器物**：粉青、梅子青及不同胎釉特征的器物。
2. **经典器型**：梅瓶、莲纹盘、鼎式炉、碗、茶具和陈设器。
3. **制作过程**：选料、练泥、拉坯、修坯、装饰、施釉、装匣、装窑、烧成和出窑。
4. **窑炉与遗址**：龙窑、窑具、窑址及考古环境。
5. **匠人与工作室**：仅收录获得授权并提供准确署名的影像。
6. **全球传播**：博物馆馆藏、考古出土和海外仿烧案例。

## English

This page does not copy online images into the repository. The first version establishes gallery categories and copyright metadata so that authorized images can be added later.

### Suggested Gallery Groups

1. **Representative wares:** Pale bluish-green, plum-green, and other documented body-glaze characteristics.
2. **Classic forms:** Meiping vase, lotus-pattern dish, tripod censer, bowl, tea ware, and display ware.
3. **Production process:** Material preparation, clay kneading, throwing, trimming, decoration, glazing, saggar loading, kiln loading, firing, and unloading.
4. **Kilns and sites:** Dragon kilns, kiln furniture, kiln sites, and archaeological environments.
5. **Artisans and workshops:** Only images with permission and accurate credit.
6. **Global circulation:** Museum collections, excavated objects, and overseas imitations.
