### 1. Shanbai: Traditional Firing of Longquan Celadon

- **Platform:** Douyin
- **Topic:** Traditional firing, classic forms, and cultural value
- **Link:** [View the Douyin work](https://v.douyin.com/_bixpg12fBg/)
- **Use in this project:** A reference for the initial structure and public narrative. The video is not downloaded or redistributed.

#### Main Points Presented

- Longquan celadon is known for a warm, jade-like glaze.
- Production includes material selection, forming, glazing, kiln loading, and firing.
- Forms such as the meiping vase, lotus-pattern dish, and censer express a restrained Eastern aesthetic.
- The tradition carries histories of craft, daily life, and cultural exchange.
- Contemporary practice combines inherited skills with modern design.
