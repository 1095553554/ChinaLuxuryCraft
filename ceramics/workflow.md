# 制作流程 | Production Workflow

## 中文

用户提供的抖音截图将流程压缩为六步：**选料、制坯、施釉、装窑、烧制、出窑**。这一表达适合公众传播，但官方非遗资料记录了更完整的过程。

### 完整流程框架

1. **原料选择与粉碎**  
   选择坯料、釉料所需原料并进行初步破碎，为后续提纯和配制做准备。

2. **淘洗与沉淀**  
   通过水洗、筛分和沉淀等处理去除不需要的粗颗粒或杂质。

3. **陈腐与练泥**  
   对泥料进行储存、陈腐和练泥，使泥料更适合成型。具体时间及操作方式因实践体系而异。

4. **成型**  
   采用手工拉坯等方式形成器物基本形态。不同器型也可能使用模具或组合成型。

5. **晾干与修坯**  
   坯体在适宜条件下逐步干燥，再修整器壁、口沿、足部和整体比例。

6. **装饰**  
   根据作品设计进行刻划、印花或其他装饰。装饰并非所有器物的必需步骤。

7. **素烧**  
   对坯体进行较低温度的预烧。具体是否素烧、顺序与次数应依据窑口和作品记录，不在本初版中统一设定。

8. **施釉**  
   通过浸釉、荡釉、涂釉或其他方式施加釉层。釉层厚度和均匀性会影响最终视觉效果。

9. **装匣、装窑**  
   将器物放入匣钵或使用相应窑具，再依据窑内空间和预期烧成效果安排窑位。

10. **烧成**  
    传统记录描述在龙窑内使用木柴烧成。烧窑过程中需要控制升温、火焰、气氛和窑内状态，操作者的经验尤为关键。

11. **冷却与出窑**  
    烧成结束后自然或按工艺要求冷却，确认安全和适宜状态后出窑。

12. **检验与记录**  
    检查釉色、器形、开裂、变形、粘连及其他结果，并将窑次、窑位、材料与结果记录下来，为后续试验和传承提供依据。

## English

The supplied Douyin screenshot condenses the process into six public-facing stages: **material selection, forming, glazing, kiln loading, firing, and unloading**. Official heritage descriptions provide a more detailed sequence.

### Extended Workflow

1. **Material selection and crushing**  
   Select and reduce raw materials for the clay body and glaze.

2. **Washing and settling**  
   Wash, screen, and settle the materials to remove unwanted coarse particles and impurities.

3. **Aging and kneading the clay**  
   Store, age, and knead the clay until it is suitable for forming. Duration and method vary by workshop.

4. **Forming**  
   Create the basic form, commonly through hand throwing. Moulding or assembled construction may also be used for particular forms.

5. **Drying and trimming**  
   Dry the body gradually and refine walls, rim, foot, and overall proportions.

6. **Decoration**  
   Apply carved, incised, impressed, or other decoration where required.

7. **Biscuit firing**  
   Carry out a preliminary firing when required by the specific process. This draft does not prescribe a universal sequence or number of firings.

8. **Glazing**  
   Apply glaze through dipping, pouring, brushing, spraying, or another documented method.

9. **Saggar loading and kiln loading**  
   Place objects in saggars or on suitable kiln furniture, then arrange them according to kiln space and expected effects.

10. **Firing**  
    Traditional records describe firing with wood in a dragon kiln. Temperature rise, flame, atmosphere, and kiln condition require close control and experienced judgment.

11. **Cooling and unloading**  
    Allow the kiln and wares to cool appropriately before unloading.

12. **Inspection and documentation**  
    Examine glaze, form, cracking, deformation, adhesion, and other outcomes. Record the firing batch, kiln position, materials, and results.
