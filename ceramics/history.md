# 历史沿革 | History

## 中文

### 历史定位

官方非遗资料将龙泉青瓷传统烧制技艺的历史概括为1700余年。龙泉青瓷并不是某一件固定器物，而是以浙江龙泉及相关窑场为中心形成并延续的青瓷生产传统。

### 发展脉络

- **早期发展**：龙泉地区依托适合制瓷的矿物原料、水源、林木燃料和交通条件，逐渐形成青瓷生产。
- **宋元时期**：龙泉窑进入最具代表性的历史阶段。粉青、梅子青等厚釉青瓷成为其重要审美标志。
- **跨区域传播**：故宫博物院“天下龙泉”展览指出，12至15世纪间，龙泉青瓷从东亚传播至西亚、非洲和欧洲部分地区，并影响了不同地区的陶瓷生产。
- **近现代复兴**：传统技艺经历衰落后逐步恢复。现代龙泉青瓷继续在材料、造型、烧成和设计表达上发展。
- **非遗保护**：2006年列入中国第一批国家级非物质文化遗产名录，2009年列入联合国教科文组织人类非物质文化遗产代表作名录。

### 历史研究应继续补充的内容

- 重要窑址及考古报告
- 宋、元、明时期典型器物的可靠断代
- 海上贸易中的出土记录与馆藏信息
- 近现代恢复生产的档案和匠人口述史

## English

### Historical Position

Official heritage sources describe the traditional firing technology of Longquan celadon as having a history of more than 1,700 years. Longquan celadon is not a single fixed object. It is a ceramic tradition formed around Longquan, Zhejiang, and related kiln communities.

### Historical Development

- **Early development:** Local mineral resources, water, timber fuel, and transport conditions supported the growth of celadon production.
- **Song and Yuan periods:** Longquan ware reached its most celebrated historical stage. Thick pale bluish-green and plum-green glazes became defining aesthetic features.
- **Transregional circulation:** The Palace Museum's *Longquan of the World* exhibition notes that from the twelfth to fifteenth centuries, Longquan celadon circulated from East Asia to West Asia, Africa, and parts of Europe, influencing ceramic production in multiple regions.
- **Modern revival:** Following a period of decline, production and transmission were gradually restored. Contemporary makers continue to explore materials, forms, firing, and design.
- **Heritage recognition:** The craft entered China's first national intangible cultural heritage list in 2006 and UNESCO's Representative List in 2009.

### Research Gaps for Later Versions

- Archaeological reports on major kiln sites
- Well-documented dating of representative Song, Yuan, and Ming objects
- Excavated evidence and museum collections related to maritime trade
- Archival records and oral histories of the modern revival
